\section{Introduction}

This revision rebuilds the S\&P\,500 regime model on four requirements: (1)~four regime axes --- momentum $\times$ volatility $\times$ turnover/liquidity $\times$ size, giving $3{\times}2{\times}2{\times}2 = 24$ bins; (2)~capital-weighted bin shares rather than headcounts; (3)~true historical index membership, never backfilled; and (4)~a deployment protocol: a zero-lookahead walk-forward forecast driving a monthly tilt portfolio with frozen weights, benchmarked against SPY and an equal-weight historical-member portfolio, with monthly equity and holdings snapshots saved as the paper track record.

A further requirement governs the whole exercise: the paper record is the \emph{prerequisite} for any real-capital deployment. Section~\ref{sec:verdict} states the verdict plainly.

\section{Data}
\label{sec:data}

\subsection{Historical S\&P\,500 membership}
Index membership comes from the public reconstruction \texttt{fja05680/sp500} (\emph{S\&P 500 Historical Components \& Changes (Updated).csv}): 2,720 snapshots/change dates from 1996-01-02 through 2026-08-18~\cite{fja05680}. This is a community reconstruction, \emph{not} an official S\&P database; change dates may lag true effective dates by days. All membership joins use the latest snapshot on or before each month-end (point-in-time).

\subsection{Prices}
Daily adjusted closes from \textbf{Yahoo Finance}~\cite{yahoo} for every ticker ever appearing in the membership file (659 raw files after alias resolution). Corporate-action aliases were validated by hand (e.g.\ \texttt{BK}$\to$\texttt{BNY}, \texttt{FB}$\to$\texttt{META}, \texttt{UTX/RTN}$\to$\texttt{RTX}). Snapshot price coverage: 2000:~57\%, 2008:~65\%, 2015:~76\%, 2021:~90\%, 2022:~94\%.

\subsection{Shares outstanding and splits}
\textbf{SEC EDGAR} companyconcept XBRL~\cite{sec} (\texttt{SharesOutstanding}, \texttt{WeightedAverageNumberOfSharesOutstanding}) provides point-in-time shares: 47,902 raw facts, 920 apparent $1{,}000\times$/$1{,}000{,}000\times$ unit errors mechanically rescaled. SEC coverage begins mid-2009; \textbf{no facts were filed by 2008-12-31}, so pre-2009 shares use earliest-filing backfill and are \emph{estimated with lookahead limitations}, flagged per-row. \textbf{Yahoo Finance} corporate-action history~\cite{yahoo} supplies 1,844 split events. \textbf{Turnover} is dollar volume over split-adjusted market cap --- a \emph{measured liquidity proxy}, not true share turnover, unless valid point-in-time shares exist.

\subsection{Panel}
\texttt{data/panel\_v2.csv}: 138,255 historical-member/month rows; 83.2\% of priced rows have cap and turnover; 80.7\% are binnable on all four axes. Figure~\ref{fig:coverage} shows coverage over time.

\section{Model}
\label{sec:model}

\subsection{State}
The capital distribution over 24 bins, $b = (m,v,\tau,s)$:
\begin{equation}
\mathbf{c}(t) \in \Delta^{23}, \qquad
c_b(t) = \frac{\sum_{i\in b}\mathrm{cap}_i(t)}{\sum_i \mathrm{cap}_i(t)} .
\end{equation}
Bin edges (Table~\ref{tab:edges}) are round absolute levels, \textbf{assumed} for interpretability, not calibrated quantiles; several low-turnover/high-volatility cells are frequently empty.

\begin{table}[h]
\centering
\caption{Four regime axes. All edges \textbf{assumed}.}
\label{tab:edges}
\begin{tabular}{lll}
\toprule
Axis & Levels & Edges \\
\midrule
Momentum $m$ & 3 & $[-1,\,0,\,0.30,\,3]$ (12--1m return) \\
Volatility $v$ & 2 & $[0,\,0.28,\,5]$ (annualized) \\
Turnover $\tau$ & 2 & $[0,\,0.008,\,10]$ (dollar vol / mcap) \\
Size $s$ & 2 & $[0,\,\$10\mathrm{B},\,\infty)$ \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{table}

\subsection{Master equation}
\begin{equation}
\frac{d\mathbf{c}}{dt} =
\underbrace{(T_{\mathrm{eff}}-I)\mathbf{c}}_{\text{drift}}
+ \underbrace{A(M)\,\mathbf{c}}_{\text{market advection}}
+ \underbrace{H(\mathbf{c})\,\mathbf{c}}_{\text{herding}}
+ \underbrace{\mathbf{e}}_{\text{entry/exit}},
\end{equation}
stepped monthly. Figure~\ref{fig:rag} shows the reaction-admissibility graph.

\paragraph{Drift (measured).} $T$ is the cap-weighted month-to-month transition matrix, estimated on 2000--2007 and expanded thereafter; $T_{\mathrm{calm}}$ for $|M|<0.05$, $T_{\mathrm{stress}}$ otherwise.

\paragraph{Market advection (mechanical).} The cross-sectional window-delta $M(t)=\bar r(t)-\bar r(t{-}12)$ shifts every stock's 12-month momentum window identically --- a pp is a pp, so $\kappa=1$ is \textbf{mechanical}. Conservative fractional remap translates each momentum lane; reflecting extreme bins accumulate mass (the spike mechanism). Participation $w_{\mathrm{eff}}=0.15$ is \textbf{assumed}.

\paragraph{Herding and sticky drift (calibrated).} The 2000--2007 grid selected $h_1=0$ and $\lambda_1=0$: neither autocatalytic herding nor sticky drift improved the pre-2008 objective. Both are \textbf{off} in rev-4; rev-3 values are not carried over as calibrated.

\paragraph{Spike-persistence damper (assumed).} At a true peak the extreme bin's mass sits deep in the tail (Mar-2021: median top-bin momentum 103\%, measured in rev-3), so a uniform donor-cell drains $\sim$2$\times$ too fast. The damper impedes extreme-bin outflow on driver-sign reversal out of a spike (gate: share $>0.5$): $\mathrm{damp}=\min((|M|/B)^p,1)$, $p=1.2$, $B$ a 6-month-decay memory of spike-building $|M|$. All damper parameters are \textbf{assumed} (rev-3 episode tuning; unidentified in the pre-2008 grid).

\subsection{Driver forecast $\widehat M_{t+1}$}
From the momentum definition, $\mathrm{mom}_i(t{+}1)-\mathrm{mom}_i(t)\approx r_i(t)-r_i(t{-}12)$, hence the \textbf{mechanical nowcast}
\begin{equation}
\widehat M(t{+}1) = R_{\mathrm{SPY}}(t) - R_{\mathrm{SPY}}(t{-}12),
\end{equation}
both known at month-end $t$ (zero lookahead). Over 2008--2022: correlation 0.50 with realized $M$, 70\% directional accuracy, RMSE 0.074 --- vs.\ $-0.02$ / 41\% for an expanding AR(1), which is discarded. The raw form overstates $|M|$ by $\sim$1.6$\times$ (measured slope 0.63, disclosed); it is used unshrunk to avoid lookahead.

\section{Conditional hindcasts}
\label{sec:hind}

These are \textbf{conditional hindcasts} (realized destination-month $M(t{+}1)$ forcing), testing the distribution mechanics --- not forecasts.

\subsection{2008--09}
Full-distribution RMSE 0.1685. Predicted bottom-lane peak 0.88 (2008-12) vs.\ realized 1.00 (2009-03): amplitude close, \textbf{three months early}. The realized 1.00 is audited saturation --- at the March-2009 trough nearly the entire cross-section sat below 0\% twelve-month momentum; no single-name outlier drives it. Post-peak decay matches within $\sim$0.05 share. The 2009 V-recovery is under-predicted: uniform-within-bin advection cannot resolve it (structural limitation).

\subsection{2020--21}
Full-distribution RMSE 0.1110. Predicted top-lane peak 0.95 (2021-04) vs.\ realized 0.92 (2021-03): within 0.03, one month late. Same-calendar-month decay (pred/real): +1\,mo 0.418/0.406, +2\,mo 0.275/0.326, +3\,mo 0.239/0.357. The damper is what aligns the decay; without it the model drains $\sim$2$\times$ too fast.

\paragraph{Mechanism before results.} Both episodes work for one reason: $M$ translates the whole momentum distribution and reflecting extreme bins accumulate the mass. The model spikes when $M$ spikes --- the distribution mechanics are slaves to the driver, which is why the \emph{forecast} is the binding constraint.

\section{Paper-trading deployment protocol}
\label{sec:paper}

\subsection{Rules}
At each month-end $t$ from 2008-01: (i)~compute $\widehat M(t{+}1)$ mechanically; (ii)~simulate one step to $\widehat{\mathbf{c}}(t{+}1)$; (iii)~tilt $\pi_b \propto \max(c_b + \gamma(\widehat c_b - c_b),0)$, $\gamma=1$ (\textbf{additive} --- the earlier exponential form was numerically unstable and is discarded); (iv)~cap-weight stocks within bins; (v)~\textbf{freeze} weights through $t{+}1$; (vi)~deduct 5\,bps one-way on turnover from correctly drifted prior weights (an early timing bug that drifted weights with future returns was found and fixed).

Benchmarks: \textbf{SPY} buy-and-hold (dividends via Yahoo adjusted closes)~\cite{yahoo}, and a \textbf{true equal-weight historical-member portfolio} (equal weights over eligible point-in-time members; the earlier equal-across-bins version is replaced). Monthly equity \emph{and} frozen per-ticker holdings snapshots are saved as first-class deployment records.

\subsection{The tilt does not work --- mechanism}
\label{sec:verdict}
The mass-flow tilt was tested to destruction. Correlation between predicted bin mass change $\Delta\widehat c_b$ and realized bin return: \textbf{0.009} (winner 0.031, loser 0.064, mid $-0.099$). A perfect-foresight variant (realized $M$, zero forecast error) reaches Sharpe 0.47 --- no better. \textbf{The failure is in the tilt mechanism, not the forecast.}

Why: advection-driven mass flows are \emph{reclassification}, not price pressure. When stocks crash they are reclassified into the loser bin --- the bin ``gains mass'' while its constituents lose money. Overweighting predicted mass-gainers overweights bins whose members just fell (or chases winners after the move). The distribution mechanics predict \emph{where capital sits}, not \emph{which bins will pay}; a return-predictive tilt needs a return model this framework does not have.

\paragraph{Verdict.} \textbf{The paper record does not support real-capital deployment.} The distribution model captures conditional spikes and the mechanical driver nowcast has genuine skill, but the portfolio built on them trails both benchmarks on every risk-adjusted metric. The prerequisite is not met. Protocol, code, and snapshots remain in place so any future tilt is judged by the same bar.

\section{Limitations and next steps}
\begin{enumerate}
\item \textbf{Return-predictive tilt} --- the binding gap: tilt on predicted bin returns via an auxiliary return model, or use the distribution forecast for crash-risk overlays rather than cross-sectional tilts.
\item \textbf{Within-bin heterogeneity} --- uniform-bin advection under-resolves V-recoveries (2009, 2022). A depth coordinate or particle-level extension is the structural fix.
\item \textbf{Pre-2009 shares} --- SEC XBRL starts mid-2009; early size/turnover bins lean on backfilled shares (flagged estimated).
\item \textbf{Membership reconstruction} --- community-sourced~\cite{fja05680}, not official S\&P; day-scale change-date lags possible.
\item \textbf{Empty cells} --- low-turnover/high-volatility bins often empty; edges kept fixed for interpretability.
\item \textbf{Damper parameters} --- assumed from rev-3 episode tuning; genuine calibration needs identified spike episodes in-sample.
\end{enumerate}
