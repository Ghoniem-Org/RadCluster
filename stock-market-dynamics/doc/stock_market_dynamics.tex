\section{Introduction}

Seven pre-registered return-forecast mechanisms were tested against 2000--2022 S\&P\,500 data. \textbf{All seven failed walk-forward validation.} This revision removes their sections and figures from the narrative and rebuilds the report around the two things that survived:

\begin{enumerate}
\item \textbf{Winner --- the systematic Buffett screen} (\S\ref{sec:buffett}). A quality/value/low-beta stock screen implementing the unlevered selection leg of ``Buffett's Alpha''~\cite{FKP2018}, paper-backtested 2009--2022: \textbf{CAGR 14.8\% vs.\ 13.6\% SPY, Sharpe 1.27 vs.\ 0.94}, FF3 alpha $+0.46\%$/month ($t=2.71$). It is the only return-generating mechanism in this project with a positive validated track record --- but it was \textbf{not} pre-registered, so it enters the paper-trading protocol as a promising backtest, not a proven edge. No real capital until the paper record holds.
\item \textbf{Validated, not a return forecaster --- the regime capital-distribution engine} (\S\ref{sec:engine}). The 24-state capital census, conditional scenario engine, and concentration/fragility monitor on clean data. Its verdict is unchanged since rev-4 and re-confirmed in rev-5: it prices \emph{states}, not \emph{returns}. A diagnostic and stress-testing instrument, never a deployment signal.
\end{enumerate}

The failed candidates are not all refuted theories --- several are weak-evidence non-replications with real design limitations --- but none of them forecasts returns in our data, so they leave the narrative. \S\ref{sec:discarded} gives each its one-line reason; the full test documents remain in the repository as honest negatives.

\section{Related work}
\label{sec:related}

\textbf{Buffett's Alpha.} Frazzini, Kabiller \& Pedersen~\cite{FKP2018} decompose Berkshire Hathaway's returns into exposures to value (HML), quality (QMJ: profitability, growth, safety, payout) and betting-against-beta (BAB), levered $\sim$1.6$\times$ through Berkshire's insurance float. \S\ref{sec:buffett} implements the unlevered stock-selection leg. Factor series come from the Ken French Data Library~\cite{FF}.

\textbf{Tested and discarded.} Brown, Howard \& Lundblad~\cite{BHL2022} find high-minus-low hedge-fund crowdedness earns $+2.8\%$/yr value-weighted ($t=6.2$) --- a compensated risk premium, not a mispricing to fade. Our public-13F proxy does not replicate it in the large-cap panel (\S\ref{sec:discarded}). Rapach, Ringgenberg \& Zhou~\cite{RRZ2016} report a negative relationship between aggregate short interest and market returns ($R^2\approx13\%$ annual in-sample); the sign reverses in our modern subsample. Greenwood, Shleifer \& You~\cite{GSY2019} find industry run-up characteristics (acceleration, rising volatility, issuance, new-firm tilt) forecast crash risk; our individual-stock adaptation does not. Lou \& Polk~\cite{LP2022} find comomentum predicts lower momentum returns; our momentum-lane comomentum has the wrong sign out of sample. The Goyal--Santa-Clara dispersion result does not replicate in the large-cap panel. Daniel \& Moskowitz~\cite{DM2016} characterize momentum crashes as a loser-optionality ``panic state'' --- a mechanism we never turned into a working forecast.

\section{Winner: the systematic Buffett screen}
\label{sec:buffett}

\subsection{Design}

\textbf{Universe:} true historical S\&P\,500 members each June (\texttt{panel\_v2.csv}, clean post-unit-fix data of \S\ref{sec:data}), rebalances June 2009--2021. Prices and membership are \textbf{measured}~\cite{yahoo,sp500}: no survivorship bias by construction.

\textbf{Scores} (cross-sectional $z$-scores, winsorized at $\pm3$, point-in-time only):

\begin{table}[h]
\centering
\caption{Buffett screen: score components.}
\label{tab:scores}
\begin{tabular}{lll}
\toprule
Component & Formula & Score \\
\midrule
Quality & $\mathrm{ROE}=\mathrm{NetIncomeLoss}/\mathrm{StockholdersEquity}$ (FY); $\mathrm{ROA}$ secondary & mean($z(\mathrm{ROE})$, $z(\mathrm{ROA})$) \\
Value & $\mathrm{B/M}=\mathrm{Equity}/\mathrm{mcap(June)}$; $\mathrm{E/P}=\mathrm{NetIncome}/\mathrm{mcap(June)}$ & mean($z(\mathrm{B/M})$, $z(\mathrm{E/P})$) \\

\end{tabular}

\textbf{Portfolio:} top 30 by composite, equal-weighted, rebalanced \textbf{annually at end of June} --- Buffett holds for years; monthly churn would be unfaithful to the method. Negative book equity excluded. Delisted holdings exit at the last available price with proceeds redistributed (\textbf{assumed}). The June-$Y$ rebalance uses the latest fiscal year ending $\geq$120 days before June 30 $Y$ (10-K filed by then in practice) --- the Fama--French end-date convention; SEC companyfacts \texttt{filed} dates are unreliable pre-2010 (restated facts carry the restating filing's date, verified on AAPL FY2007), so the end-date rule governs point-in-time status~\cite{sec}.

\textbf{Data provenance.} Fundamentals (NetIncomeLoss, StockholdersEquity, Assets): \textbf{measured} (SEC EDGAR XBRL companyfacts, 10-K facts only, USD~\cite{sec}); 584/585 tickers fetched, 19 absurd values rejected by absolute-anchor bounds. Coverage: XBRL mandate began 2009 (FY2008+: 424--569 tickers); the June-2008 rebalance had only 3 eligible stocks and was \textbf{skipped} --- the backtest starts June 2009. 11 tickers (e.g.\ XOM, PNC, BSX) have 10-Q-only facts and are excluded: a documented gap, not filled. FF factors: \textbf{measured} (Ken French Data Library, 202608 CRSP vintage~\cite{FF}); AQR's QMJ/BAB downloads are bot-walled (verified 2026-09-28) --- not used, no proxy substituted.

It wins the down years (2011 \textbf{+16.8 / +1.9}, 2018 \textbf{+1.9 / $-$4.6}, 2022 \textbf{$-$7.5 / $-$20.0}) and lags raging bulls (2020 \textbf{$-$0.6 / +18.3}, missing mega-cap tech --- as Buffett himself did). Holdings pass the smell test (MO, CL, PEP, BRK-B, WMT, MCD, KMB across years). The notable analytical finding: the HML loading is \textbf{zero} --- the quality component (high ROE) neutralizes the value tilt, so this behaves as a \textbf{quality + low-beta} screen, not deep value.

\subsection{Limitations and paper-trading status}

\textbf{Not pre-registered} --- design choices (top 30, equal weight, June rebalance, composite formula) are standard-academic but were the builder's, so researcher degrees of freedom apply: weaker evidence than the pre-registered negative tests. No float leverage; no crisis deal-flow; no QMJ/BAB attribution; 11 tickers excluded; no transaction costs modeled (53\% annual one-way turnover $\approx$ 0.1--0.3\%/yr at institutional rates, \textbf{assumed}); one 14-year window dominated by the post-2009 bull market; 2020 shows the known failure mode.

The screen enters the \textbf{paper-trading protocol}: frozen design, live tracking vs.\ SPY. \textbf{No real capital until the paper record holds;} then a small pilot allocation.

\section{Validated: the regime capital-distribution engine}
\label{sec:engine}

\subsection{Master equation and reaction graph}

Capital is distributed over 24 states (3 momentum $\times$ 2 volatility $\times$ 2 turnover $\times$ 2 size), capital-weighted, on true historical membership. The state vector obeys the cluster master equation

\begin{equation}
\frac{d\mathbf{c}}{dt} = \mathbf{P} + \mathbf{S}\mathbf{J} - \mathbf{D}\mathbf{c},
\end{equation}

with $\mathbf{c}$ the 24-bin capital shares, $\mathbf{P}$ the index-entry source, $\mathbf{S}\mathbf{J}$ the reaction-flux stoichiometry, and $\mathbf{D}\mathbf{c}$ the exit sink. Figure~\ref{fig:rag} shows the full reaction-admissibility graph: momentum advection (red/blue, mechanical, $\kappa=1.0$) and volatility drift (gray dashed, measured $24\times24$ $T$).

\subsection{Data: the share-unit integrity fix}
\label{sec:data}

Filed share counts carried consistent $10^3$--$10^6\times$ unit errors the jump-based cleaner missed --- including a backfill reading Sempra Energy at \textbf{\$5{,}000\,trillion}, flipping the measured top-lane share between 0.999 and 0.002 month to month~\cite{sec}. The repair (\texttt{fix\_share\_units.py}): per-ticker segmentation at $>20\times$ split-adjusted jumps, each segment checked against two absolute anchors --- market cap in [\$200M, \$4T] and average-daily turnover in [0.02\%, 100\%] (dollar volume is \textbf{measured}~\cite{yahoo}). 28 segments rescaled by $10^{\pm3}, 10^{\pm6}$; 17 dropped, never invented. Verification: total binnable cap 2007-12 \textbf{\$10.2T} (S\&P\,500 $\approx$ \$13T), 2021-12 \textbf{\$41.3T} ($\approx$ \$40T). Cap-weighting is multiplicative in the share count --- on clean data the conditional hindcasts improve to RMSE \textbf{0.0899} (2008--09) and \textbf{0.0714} (2020--21), with the 2020--21 peak amplitude exact.

\subsection{Scenario engine and fragility monitor}

The forecastable object is $\mathbf{c}(t)$, not returns. A 200-scenario ensemble from 2022-12 (Dirichlet-drawn $T$, \textbf{estimated} Kish $N_{\mathrm{eff}}$ 109--1825/bin; AR(1) market driver, \textbf{estimated}; VIX-regime Markov chain, \textbf{measured}) gives 12-month-ahead top-lane share \textbf{median 0.239, 90\% interval [0.106, 0.503]}; HHI [0.110, 0.175] (Figure~\ref{fig:ens}). The interval width is the finding: one-year concentration is dominated by driver and transition uncertainty.

\textbf{Standing verdict:} the regime engine does not forecast returns and must not drive capital deployment. Its legitimate uses are regime census, conditional scenario analysis, and concentration/fragility monitoring.

\section{Discarded candidates}
\label{sec:discarded}

Seven return-forecast mechanisms were tested with pre-registered designs and walk-forward validation. All failed; their full test documents remain in the repository as honest negatives. One-line reasons:

\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{Rev-4 walk-forward tilt} --- predicted mass-flow vs.\ realized bin return: correlation 0.009; tilt Sharpe 0.48 vs.\ SPY 0.64. Bin-boundary migration is reclassification, not price pressure.
\item \textbf{Concentration $\to$ returns} --- threshold-linear $t=-0.41$, walk-forward corr $-0.045$; quintile spreads statistically zero ($|t|<0.6$), non-monotone. Honest negative.
\item \textbf{Dispersion $\to$ returns} (Goyal--Santa-Clara style) --- no replication in the large-cap panel; all out-of-sample $R^2$ negative.
\item \textbf{Comomentum} (Lou--Polk) --- winner-lane slope $-0.46$ (NW $t=-0.27$) with the wrong sign on the winner component; walk-forward corr $-0.095$, OOS $R^2=-0.082$.
\item \textbf{Short interest} (Rapach--Ringgenberg--Zhou) --- the published \emph{negative} relationship reversed sign in the modern subsample (12-month $\beta=+0.42$, NW $t=+1.31$); not accepted post hoc.
\item \textbf{Bubble characteristics} (Greenwood--Shleifer--You, adapted to individual stocks) --- crash logit pseudo-$R^2=0.0134$; acceleration $p=0.138$ with the wrong sign; issuance (one of the paper's strongest predictors) unavailable. Weak evidence, not a refutation.
\item \textbf{Hedge-fund crowding} (Brown--Howard--Lundblad) --- public-13F proxy non-replication: all three pass criteria failed; the one marginal signal (hf\_own 3-month, $t=+2.13$) collapsed to $t=+1.22$ under size control; CUSIP$\to$ticker mapping covered only 25.6\% of CUSIPs.
\end{itemize}

\section{Next steps}

\begin{enumerate}
\item \textbf{Paper-trade the Buffett screen} (frozen design, live tracking vs.\ SPY). Real capital only if the paper record holds; then a small pilot.
\item \textbf{Keep the regime engine as a diagnostic}: census, scenario intervals, fragility monitor. No return-forecast development without return-side economics (earnings, flows) --- reclassification arithmetic is exhausted.
\item The discarded candidates' test documents stay in the repo as the permanent record of what was tried and why it was dropped.
\end{enumerate}
